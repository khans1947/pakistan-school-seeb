Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Pakistan School Seeb, Al Seeb, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman

1. Who we are

Pakistan School Seeb (“we”, “the school”) processes personal data to provide education services, admissions, and parent communication.

2. Data we collect

Application forms may include student name, date of birth, grade, guardian contact details, previous school records, and uploaded identity/academic documents. Inquiry forms collect name, email, phone, and message content.

3. How we use data

We use data to process admissions, respond to inquiries, manage bookstore/uniform requests, send application status updates, and meet legal/educational obligations in Oman.

4. Storage & security

Data may be stored in secure cloud services (e.g. Firebase/Google Cloud) with access limited to authorised school staff. You should use strong admin credentials and restrict API keys by domain.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with regulatory/education authorities when required by Omani law, or with service providers acting under our instructions (hosting, email).

6. Retention

Application records are retained as required for school administration and legal compliance, then securely deleted or archived.

7. Your rights

Parents/guardians may request access, correction, or deletion of application data by contacting the school office: principal.seeb@pakistanschool.edu.om · +968 2446 4345.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies/local storage for language preference, theme, and form progress. Optional analytics (if enabled) is disclosed in the cookie banner.
