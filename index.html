Pakistan School Seeb

Inaugurated on 21st August 2014, Pakistan School Seeb was established to extend quality, affordable education to families in Al Seeb and surrounding areas. Operating under the Board of Directors of Pakistan Schools Oman and the supervision of the Ministry of Education, Sultanate of Oman.

With more than 1,300 multinational students and classes from Kindergarten through Grade 12, we offer both FBISE (Islamabad) and Cambridge pathways, supported by modern classrooms, smartboards, science labs, and vibrant extracurricular programmes.
