Pathways

Curriculum Pathways

FBISE Stream

Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Islamabad. SSC (9–10) and HSSC (11–12) with Science and Arts groups.

  • National curriculum alignment
  • Recognised across Pakistan & GCC
  • Strong board exam preparation
  • Subject specialists for senior grades

Cambridge Stream

Cambridge International pathways including IGCSE options for students aiming at global universities.

  • International recognition
  • Skills-focused assessment
  • Flexible subject combinations
  • University counselling support

Junior Foundation

Kindergarten through Grade 5 with emphasis on literacy, numeracy, inquiry, and character development.

  • Activity-based learning
  • Smartboard-enabled classrooms
  • Specialist KG faculty
  • Continuous assessment
